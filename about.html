Background

I've been traveling and writing independently for nine years, across 63 countries. I've made mistakes most travel bloggers would never admit to — bad border crossings, overpriced accommodation, wrong-season timing.

Travel Verge is the resource I wish had existed: specific, honest, and written by someone who went there on their own money. No sponsored stays, no destination board partnerships.
