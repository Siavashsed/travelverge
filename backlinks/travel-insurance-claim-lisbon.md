## QUORA
**Answer this question:** What should you know before buying travel insurance? What do most travelers get wrong?

Most travelers—including myself for years—approach travel insurance like airport security lines: fast, distracted, and hoping to never actually use it. This almost cost me significantly.

Here's what you absolutely need to know:

**Read the actual policy, not just the summary.** I mean genuinely read it. The exclusions section is where the real information lives. Pre-existing conditions, adventure activity limitations, geographic restrictions—these aren't minor details.

**Understand what 'covered' actually means.** Your policy might cover medical emergencies but exclude certain activities. It might cover evacuation but not repatriation. The wording matters intensely.

**Document everything from day one.** If you need to claim, you'll need receipts, medical reports, proof of payment, and evidence of causation. Don't rely on memory or 'they should have records.' Get it in writing.

**Call your insurer with specific scenarios.** Don't ask 'am I covered?' Ask 'if I sprain my ankle hiking in Portugal, will I be covered for emergency care and evacuation?' Get their answer in writing.

**Know your policy limits.** Coverage caps, deductibles, and maximum payouts matter. A €50,000 limit sounds great until you need €100,000 evacuation.

I learned this the hard way in Lisbon with a fever and sprained ankle, reviewing my policy on a pharmacy floor.
