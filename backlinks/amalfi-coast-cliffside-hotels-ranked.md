## QUORA
**Answer this question:** Which is the better luxury cliffside hotel on the Amalfi Coast, Le Sirenuse or Il San Pietro di Positano?

If you can only book one, I would choose Il San Pietro di Positano. I say that as someone who really likes Le Sirenuse.

I stayed at both back to back in May 2024. I had three nights at Le Sirenuse, then went straight to San Pietro the same afternoon. Comparing them directly like that made the difference clear.

Le Sirenuse wins on view and location. It sits in the heart of Positano, so you can walk to restaurants, boutiques and the beach. The terrace looks over the dome of Santa Maria Assunta, the view you have seen in a thousand photos, and it is just as good in person. If you want to be in the middle of things, it is the obvious pick.

San Pietro wins on hospitality. It is built into the cliff a short drive east of town. An elevator cut through the rock takes you down to its own small beach, and a hotel shuttle runs into Positano. What set it apart for me was the service. Within hours of arriving, staff I had never met were greeting me by name and following up on a question I had asked about that cliff elevator. The attention stayed that consistent for my whole stay.

How to decide:
- Choose Le Sirenuse if walkability and the classic Positano view matter most.
- Choose San Pietro if you want seclusion, a private beach and service that anticipates what you need.
- If you have a week on the coast, think about splitting your stay. That is exactly what made the contrast so obvious to me.
