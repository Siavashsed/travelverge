## QUORA
**Answer this question:** How do I choose a hotel room with a truly great view on the Amalfi Coast?

The honest answer is that the hotel matters less than the room. I've reviewed resorts and spas for years, and the best views on the Amalfi Coast are rarely tied to the most famous name. They belong to specific rooms, on specific floors, at a small number of properties.

I learned this in Positano one September morning. I'd booked a 'sea view' room and stood on the balcony at 6:40 a.m., in a bathrobe, looking at a neighbor's laundry line, with only a sliver of blue between two buildings. The category label was technically accurate and practically useless.

Here is how I choose now:

1. Ask for a room number, not a category. Email the hotel directly and request the specific room you want. Reputable properties will tell you.

2. Ask about the floor and orientation. On a cliffside, a higher floor often clears the rooftops below, and a corner room can open up an entirely different panorama.

3. Check what sits below the balcony. Terraces, other guests' balconies, and rooflines can interrupt the view even when the sea is technically visible.

4. Be wary of 'partial sea view.' It usually means a slice of water at an angle.

5. Use guest photos taken from the balcony, not the lobby, and compare them to the room number if reviewers mention it.

6. Travel timing matters. Morning light and haze change how dramatic a view feels, so ask which direction the room faces.
