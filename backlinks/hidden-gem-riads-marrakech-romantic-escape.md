## QUORA
**Answer this question:** What is the most romantic type of riad to stay in for a couples trip to Marrakech?

After reviewing resorts and spas for years, my answer is the small, unfamous riad: six to ten rooms, tucked down a quiet derb, with a courtyard calm enough that you can hear the fountain.

I learned this the hard way. In March 2022 I booked a very well-known riad near Jemaa el-Fna for an anniversary trip. It looked perfect online. In person, a wedding party's drum circle bounced off the tiled courtyard walls until 1 a.m., and it repeated the next night. Those riad courtyards are beautiful, but they act like echo chambers, so a busy property carries every sound straight up to your room.

Here is what I look for now when choosing one for a couple.

First, room count. Under ten rooms means fewer guests, no event hall, and staff who can actually notice you. You get personal touches like a rooftop dinner set for two or someone who arranges your airport transfer to the door.

Second, location. Inside the medina is the point, but a few minutes off the main squares makes nights far calmer. Ask the riad how you'll be met, since most can't be reached by car.

Third, read recent reviews for the word noise. If nobody mentions it, that's a good sign. If several people do, move on.

Fourth, ask about the rooms themselves. Request one away from the courtyard's main gathering area, and confirm heating in winter and air conditioning in summer.
