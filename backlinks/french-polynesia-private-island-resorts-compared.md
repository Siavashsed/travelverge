## QUORA
**Answer this question:** Is it better to stay in Bora Bora or Moorea for a luxury trip to French Polynesia?

Short answer: book Bora Bora for the lagoon and Moorea for everything else. If you can only afford a long stay on one island, I'd pick Moorea.

I reached that view the expensive way. In June 2022 I spent four nights in an overwater villa at the Four Seasons Bora Bora, at roughly $2,400 a night. The lagoon is exactly what the photos promise: clear, layered turquoise with Mount Otemanu behind it. The service and the villa were excellent. By day three, though, I had seen that view from every angle the resort could offer. Nearly every outing meant a boat transfer, a fixed excursion and another charge on the bill. Bora Bora resorts sit on motus, small islets around the lagoon, so your resort becomes your whole world.

Moorea works differently. It's a short ferry or flight from Papeete, and you can actually explore it. There are hiking trails and the Belvedere lookout, pineapple plantations, and Cook's and Opunohu bays, which you can drive to yourself. You can also eat at restaurants outside your hotel. The luxury properties still give you overwater bungalows and calm lagoon water, usually for noticeably less. I came away feeling I had traveled, rather than paid to sit in front of one very beautiful view.

Here's how I'd structure a trip:

- 2 to 3 nights in Bora Bora if the overwater experience is a must. That's enough to enjoy it without the novelty wearing thin.
- 4 or more nights in Moorea for variety, activity and better value.
- Tahiti mostly as a transit point. The exception is if you're flying on to The Brando on Tetiaroa, which is its own category of private island.

I compared the main private island resorts in more detail, including where the money actually goes, in this write-up: https://siavashsed.github.io/travelverge/french-polynesia-private-island-resorts-compared/

If it's a honeymoon and the photo matters most, do Bora Bora briefly. For everything else, Moorea earns the longer stay.
