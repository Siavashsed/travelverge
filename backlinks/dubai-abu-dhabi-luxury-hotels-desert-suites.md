## QUORA
**Answer this question:** Is it better to splurge on a luxury hotel in Dubai or Abu Dhabi?

After years of reviewing resorts and spas firsthand, my honest answer is that the city matters less than what you spend the money on. If you can only splurge once in the UAE, put it into the desert and the dining room rather than the tallest suite.

Here's the experience that taught me. In March 2024 I paid a small fortune for a skyline suite in Dubai. It was polished, the view was dramatic, and I'd happily describe it as a very expensive room in a very busy city. Two nights later I was at Qasr Al Sarab, roughly 200 kilometers southwest of Abu Dhabi. At 5:40 a.m. I walked onto a dune with a thermos of cardamom coffee and had an entire horizon to myself. That is something no altitude premium can buy you.

So how I'd think about it:

1. Dubai is best for energy, variety of restaurants and spectacle. Splurge here on a great meal or a standout spa treatment rather than the highest floor.

2. Abu Dhabi and its desert region are best for space, quiet and a sense of place. This is where the money feels like it buys something rare.

3. Judge a property by service ratios, privacy and the kitchen, not by its ranking on a 'tallest' or 'biggest' list.

4. If you have time, split the trip: a night or two in the city for the show, then the desert for the part you'll remember.
